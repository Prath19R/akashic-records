Nahida chibi sprite assets
==========================

The PNGs are individual crops from the supplied sprite sheet. The white
sheet background and connected light-grey guide marks were removed; enclosed
character details were preserved.

Folders:
- 01_movement: five running frames and three walking-left frames
- 02_physics_events: impact, recover, ready/land, and three falling/tumbling
  poses
- 03_chat_interaction: thinking, pondering, ask/invite, loading, excited, and
  answering poses
- 04_exit: waving/leaving and dissolving-out poses

Source-sheet limitations:
- No separate neutral idle or get-up frame is present.
- Loading is represented by one frame only.
- The sheet does not provide animation timing or frame metadata.
